Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 November 2026

For questions about who operates the service, how your information is used, or your privacy rights, contact support@wearesparklab.com.

Money, Sorted is provided by SparkLab. For privacy questions, email support@wearesparklab.com.

Information you enter

We store your email address and the financial information you choose to enter, such as income, expenses, payment dates, and card names, balances, limits and payments. Money, Sorted does not connect to your bank and does not ask for a full payment-card number.

How it is used

We use this information to sign you in, save your entries across devices, and calculate the totals and dates shown in the app. Supabase provides authentication and database storage; Vercel hosts the app. Your browser stores a session so you can remain signed in.

Who can see it

Your signed-in account can access its own Money, Sorted records. The database uses Row Level Security to separate accounts. SparkLab administrators may need access to operate the service or respond to support requests.

Keeping and deleting data

Your Money, Sorted records remain until you remove them or ask us to help. The app’s “Delete my Money, Sorted data” action removes your live financial records. It leaves your shared SparkLab login in place because that login may be used by other SparkLab apps. To request deletion of the shared login or a copy of your data, email support@wearesparklab.com.

Older copies may remain temporarily in provider backups. The exact backup retention period is being confirmed; ask us at the support address above for the current position.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the service changes. Questions or corrections can be sent to support@wearesparklab.com.
